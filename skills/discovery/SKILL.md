# Discovery without a questionnaire

Establish the intended outcome from discussion, source and inspected evidence. Ask one consequential question when genuinely needed; do not ask again for settled decisions. This is not a new questionnaire or planning suite.

Inspect existing rules/context first. Use a bounded experiment only if it can change the decision: question, comparator when useful, sufficient-evidence criteria, permitted inputs/effects and relevant checks. Honor a user-agreed budget; do not invent quotas or a benchmark project. At blockers consider a cheaper safe alternative or stopping with honest uncertainty. A failed or inconclusive experiment can be useful evidence. Park unrelated improvements.

Recommend Direct, Developer or Team with a reason when helpful. Choose proportionate execution mechanics without requiring a mode-selection ritual. A clear implementation request authorizes ordinary reversible development once consequential gaps are resolved; proposals and readiness alone do not. Do not require a separate Agreement approval of already requested work. Existing explicit permissions, exclusions and limits remain binding.

Supply purpose, constraints, acceptance and relevant source/rules pointers. No mandatory reading manifests, snapshots or historical rereading. Excerpts must retain governing constraints. Use native codemode for bounded retrieval; logs stay on disk.

Scripts first for deterministic extraction/counting. A read-only Scout can answer a focused question using supplied accessible local sources. Its packet records actual discovery authority, rules/exclusions and a cited result contract; metadata is not private-data consent. Worker tools do not widen permissions. See ../../docs/workflows/scout-role-routing.md for the API.

Do not interfere with other applications; isolate live-side-effect tests and report gaps. Commit/install/delete/push/publish and new private-data or trust access need applicable authority. Narrate findings and decisions, not every call. Retain concise Munin handoffs without secrets; check current state.
