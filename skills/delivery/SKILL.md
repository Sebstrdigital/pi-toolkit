# Outcome-driven delivery

A clear request to execute an understood task is authority for its ordinary reversible development. Do not require the user to select a mode and then approve a separate Agreement. Advice, a draft proposal or vague readiness is not execution authority. Resolve consequential gaps once; preserve explicit limits and exclusions.

Use a concise task: purpose, intended behavior, acceptance, relevant rules and boundaries. Conversation, an issue or existing JSON can carry it; no mandatory record or snapshot suite. Templates are optional. No mandatory reading manifests or read receipts. Read relevant source on demand; excerpts must not omit governing constraints. Keep logs on disk. Reuse unchanged context/evidence.

The lead owns implementation, diagnosis, repair, validation and outcome acceptance. Red checks and independent findings trigger useful repairs without customer reapproval. Ordinary compiler/test/harness corrections are implementation, not repair tokens. Do not invent repair quotas, per-role budgets or cumulative accounting ceremonies. User-agreed limits remain binding, including old narrow packets. Diagnose repeated defects and change strategy; stop for demonstrated no progress, exhausted explicit limits or consequential new authority.

File lists are pointers unless explicitly restricted. Include the minimum supporting files needed for the approved outcome; do not add features or cross exclusions. A materially new behavior, trust boundary, private-data access or required-check change needs a decision, not every new filename.

Read references/delegation.md before delegating. Choose useful roles and context lifetimes; no mandatory fresh builder/validator/reviewer cycle per slice. Independent checks/review should fit the risk and any requested gates. One final integrated check/review can cover related changes; renew affected evidence after repair, not assurance-only full reruns.

The tmux adapter dispatches safely and permits concurrent workers in distinct linked worktrees. Same-workspace ownership remains exclusive, including pause and question waits. Do not run parallel writers in one checkout. Workers do not nest. Reconcile partial changes, pins and resources before continuation or bounded replacement; never blindly replay side effects or reset explicit limits.

Do not control unrelated applications or stop the app under development without authority. Isolate live-side-effect tests and verify effective test-host isolation before sensitive checks. New private-data access, destructive actions and publication need applicable authority. Finish with the exact useful candidate, actual/reused checks, a consolidated manual checklist and honest gaps. The lead alone accepts; a worker result or green test is not product acceptance or permission to publish.
